Paver Repair & Sealing in Florida

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Paver Match

Short answer: Sunken or shifted pavers usually point to base or drainage problems; re-leveling means lifting pavers, fixing the base and relaying them. Sealing is optional maintenance.[1]

Check whether your driveway apron sits in the public right-of-way and whether your city counts pavers toward impervious-surface limits. Both change what permits you need.

What to know

  • If the problem is in the right-of-way apron, the same right-of-way rules apply to repairs.
  • Fernandina Beach says the city is not responsible for maintaining pavers placed in its right-of-way.
