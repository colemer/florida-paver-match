Pavers services

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Paver Match

  • Paver Driveways: A paver driveway usually needs two approvals: anything your city requires for the work on your lot, and a right-of-way or encroachment permit for the apron between the property line and the street..
  • Paver Patios: Patios add impervious area, which some cities cap or permit.
  • Paver Pool Decks: Pool-deck pavers are often installed over or in place of old concrete decks.
  • Paver Repair & Sealing: Sunken or shifted pavers usually point to base or drainage problems; re-leveling means lifting pavers, fixing the base and relaying them.
