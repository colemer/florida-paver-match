Pavers in Largo: what's different here

Largo paver jobs run through the Largo Civic Access Portal, and the city's fence rules (every fence needs a permit, 2 feet back from sidewalks) often shape where a new patio edge can go.

  • Largo's driveway permit covers residential property only and needs an online application, a survey or site plan with dimensions, and a description of the material.[3]
  • Largo requires a building permit for every fence install, repair or replacement, and fences must sit at least 2 feet from sidewalks, which affects paver edges along a fence line.[4]

Storm and flooding history (drainage matters for pavers)

Paver base and joint sand depend on drainage. These are the recent storms that flooded or soaked this area.

  • Hurricane Milton (October 9, 2024): The Largo McKay Creek rain gauge recorded 16.88 inches, and a CoCoRaHS site 1.5 miles south-southwest of Largo caught 12.11 inches.[1]
  • Hurricane Helene (September 26, 2024): Surge inundation of 5 to 7 feet above ground ran along Pinellas County's Gulf coast, 12 people in the county drowned, and at least 419 homes were destroyed across Pinellas and Hillsborough.[2]

Flood risk and FEMA maps

In Largo, creek and street flooding from heavy rain is the recent test (16.88 inches at the McKay Creek gauge in Milton), and Helene showed how far Gulf surge reaches into Pinellas County. Check your parcel on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center.[1][2][5]

Who issues permits in Largo?

City of Largo Building Services (Largo Civic Access Portal). Phone: City 727-587-6700; Planner on Call 727-587-6749 ext. 7301. Permits are filed through the Largo Civic Access Portal (largo.com/lcap); email permits@largo.com.[3][4]

Rules for pavers differ by jurisdiction: some require a building permit for anything that adds impervious area, and work in the public right-of-way (most driveway aprons) usually needs a separate right-of-way or encroachment permit. Confirm with the office before work starts.

Largo pavers FAQs

What does a Largo driveway permit require?

An online application, a survey or site plan showing the driveway's location and dimensions, and a description of what it will be built from. It is for residential property only.[3]

Where do I apply?

Through the Largo Civic Access Portal (largo.com/lcap). Email permits@largo.com if your permit type is not listed.[3]

Does a fence affect where my pavers can go?

Largo requires a permit for every fence and keeps fences at least 2 feet from sidewalks, so plan paver edges and fence posts together.[4]

How much rain did Milton bring to Largo?

16.88 inches at the McKay Creek gauge, and 12.11 inches 1.5 miles south-southwest of the city.[1]
