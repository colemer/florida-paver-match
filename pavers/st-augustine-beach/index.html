Pavers in St. Augustine Beach: what's different here

Storm and flooding history (drainage matters for pavers)

Paver base and joint sand depend on drainage. These are the recent storms that flooded or soaked this area.

  • Hurricane Matthew (October 7, 2016): Surge reached the St. Augustine Beach sea wall early in the morning and water flowed across Anastasia Island about 2.5 feet above ground.[1][2]
  • Hurricane Ian (September 29, 2022): The St. Augustine Beach Fishing Pier recorded a peak storm tide of +7.43 feet NAVD. FDEP rated the developed part of Anastasia Island, including St. Augustine Beach, as major dune and beach erosion (Condition IV).[3]
  • Hurricane Nicole (November 10, 2022): Nicole worsened Ian's erosion; FDEP measured about 1.48 million cubic yards of sand lost above mean high water from St. Augustine to Matanzas Inlet across the two storms.[4]

Flood risk and FEMA maps

Homes here can take water from both sides: ocean overwash on the east and the Matanzas River and marsh on the west. Ocean-side lots may sit in coastal high-hazard flood zones with stricter rebuild rules; confirm the zone for an address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center before planning repairs.[1][7][8]

Who issues permits in St. Augustine Beach?

City of St. Augustine Beach Building & Zoning. Phone: 904-471-8758. The city runs its own building department; applications and plans are submitted to the city, not St. Johns County. Parts of Anastasia Island outside city limits (Butler Beach, Crescent Beach) are permitted by the county.[5][6]

Rules for pavers differ by jurisdiction: some require a building permit for anything that adds impervious area, and work in the public right-of-way (most driveway aprons) usually needs a separate right-of-way or encroachment permit. Confirm with the office before work starts.

What does it cost?

Angi's 2026 data puts the U.S. average paver driveway at about $12,000, with most homeowners paying $6,000 to $18,000 (roughly $10 to $30 per square foot installed).[9] These are national figures; we have not found public local cost data. How Florida jobs differ.
