How much does a paver driveway cost? (2026 national data)

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Paver Match

Short answer

Short answer: Angi's 2026 data puts the average paver driveway at about $12,000, with most homeowners paying $6,000 to $18,000. Add local permit fees, for example $36 for a St. Johns County driveway connection permit or $150 for a Clay County driveway change or upgrade.

  1. National figures
  2. Local permit fees

National figures

About $12,000 on average; most pay $6,000 to $18,000; roughly $10 to $30 per square foot installed.[1]

Local permit fees

St. Johns County driveway connection permit: $36.

Clay County driveway permit: $175 new, $150 change or upgrade, $75 existing or exemption.[2][3]

Cost ranges

Angi's 2026 data puts the U.S. average paver driveway at about $12,000, with most homeowners paying $6,000 to $18,000 (roughly $10 to $30 per square foot installed).[1]
