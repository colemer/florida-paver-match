What happens next

  1. We review your request. We aim to review requests within a few hours during the day, every day. Overnight requests are reviewed the next morning.
  2. Who contacts you. No partner companies are listed yet, so we do not share any request with any company. We (Cole Merlino, Florida Paver Match) read your request and reply to you ourselves, by email (or by phone or text only if you checked the box). A company added later will never receive a request sent before it was named here.
  3. You choose. Hire whoever you prefer, or no one.
While you wait: Measure the area (length x width), note whether a sidewalk crosses the driveway, and find your lot survey if you have one. Permit offices often ask for it.

Florida Paver Match is a free referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform paver installation or any construction work. With the homeowner’s consent, we pass a request to up to 3 independent local companies named on our form (none are listed yet, so no requests are shared). Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted. Each contractor is responsible for its own licensing, insurance, pricing and work. You are never obligated to hire anyone.
