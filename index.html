Frequently asked questions

Is this a paver company?

No. Florida Paver Match is a free referral service. With your consent, we can pass your request to up to 3 independent local paver companies named on our form.

Do I need a permit for a paver driveway in Northeast Florida?

Usually at least for the apron in the right-of-way. St. Johns County charges $36 for a driveway connection permit; Jacksonville Beach uses a Revocable Encroachment Permit; Clay County requires a driveway permit for paver upgrades connecting to county right-of-way.[1][2][3]

What does a paver driveway cost?

Angi's 2026 national average is about $12,000, with most homeowners paying $6,000 to $18,000.[4]
